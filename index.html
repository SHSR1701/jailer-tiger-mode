How to play: Move with ← / → or A / D. Jump with Space / W / ↑. Hold J to strike; every third strike is a stronger kick. Hits fill the SWAG meter. At 100%, press K for Tiger Mode: a room-clearing sunglasses move. Q (or the Narasimha button) marks the toughest visible enemy, preferring Varman, then fires three visible sniper shots for 2 damage each after a lock-on delay. If a target falls, remaining shots find the next toughest enemy. E (or the Mathew button) calls a bodyguard who runs in, blocks up to three melee hits or projectiles, and punches nearby enemies for seven seconds after arrival. Stay behind him; he is not a whole-screen shield. Narasimha recharges in 18 seconds and Mathew in 24 seconds. Calls without a visible enemy cost nothing. Both refresh at each round. Neither call makes you invulnerable. Jump over attacks, watch for red warning marks, and defeat all three rounds. P / Escape pauses. Touch buttons support simultaneous movement and attacks. Each new round restores two hearts. Your best score is saved on this device when browser storage is available.

Characters: Rajinikanth plays retired jailer “Tiger” Muthuvel Pandian; Vinayakan plays idol smuggler Varman. Shiva Rajkumar's Narasimha and Mohanlal's Mathew appear as allies. Silver hair and shades, dark studded clothes, a patterned shirt, and Varman's curls inform these original pixel interpretations. The arena story and assist mechanics are invented for this game. Sources: film cast and story · the cameos and their signature looks.

Sound: A compact chiptune arrangement of the hook from “Hukum – Thalaivar Alappara,” composed by Anirudh Ravichander. The hook is played on pulse-wave leads with triangle bass and noise drums at 102 BPM. This is a simplified instrumental hook loop, not the complete song or original recording; no vocals are included. Melody reference: Key Speaks’ Hukum piano tutorial. Listen to the original release. Audio starts after a tap. Use Sound to mute.
